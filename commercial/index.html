Which Commercial Restoration Services Do We Offer?

Every commercial loss starts with the same question: what happened and what's at risk? These services cover the full range of commercial damage we respond to across the Tulsa metro.

  • Commercial water damage restoration extraction and drying for offices, retail, warehouses and multi-family buildings
  • Flood damage restoration Category 3 floodwater cleanup, contaminated-material removal and structural disinfection
  • Sewage backup cleanup restroom overflows, floor drain backups and lower-level unit contamination
  • Mold remediation containment and removal under IICRC S520 guidelines for commercial spaces
  • Storm damage repair emergency tarping, board-up and interior drying after wind, hail and tornado damage
  • Fire damage restoration board-up, water extraction, soot cleaning, deodorization and rebuild
  • Smoke and soot cleanup surface cleaning, HVAC assessment and odor removal

How Do We Work With Businesses During Restoration?

Commercial restoration operates under different constraints than residential operating hours, tenant coordination, business continuity, contents protection and multi-party insurance structures all factor into the plan.

Scope walk-through with your facilities contact before any work begins. A project lead walks every affected area, identifies at-risk building systems (electrical, HVAC, data, elevators) and sets the drying plan, work zones, crew schedule and timeline with your facilities manager or property management company.

Sectioned work zones so unaffected areas can remain open where safe. The goal is always to minimize business interruption closing only what must close, for only as long as necessary.

After-hours scheduling for loud or disruptive work when possible. Extraction, selective demolition and heavy equipment placement are scheduled around your operating hours.

Contents protection inventory, equipment, files and fixtures are photographed, inventoried and either dried on site, covered and protected or packed out and stored off-site. The contents inventory supports the contents portion of your insurance claim.

Carrier-ready documentation moisture maps with readings at every point, daily drying logs, equipment placement and runtime records, before/during/after photos and an itemized scope formatted for commercial adjusters and risk managers. Frequently Asked Questions

Can you start after business hours?

+
Yes (918) 395-9320 is answered around the clock, 365 days a year. Commercial emergencies are prioritized because business-interruption costs compound every hour.

Do you coordinate with property managers and adjusters?

+
Yes we can walk the loss with property managers, building owners, adjusters and third-party consultants. Documentation is structured for each party's needs: per-unit breakdowns for multi-family, per-suite for multi-tenant offices, itemized scopes for carriers.

Can part of the building stay open during restoration?

+
Often with properly sectioned work zones, physical barriers and scheduling of disruptive work after hours. If an area must close for safety (contamination, electrical hazard, structural concern), we explain why and for how long.

Do you handle multi-unit apartment losses?

+
Yes each affected unit gets separate moisture maps, drying logs and photo documentation, so property managers and carriers can process each unit's scope independently.
